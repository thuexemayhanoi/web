About Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu

Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu is a motorbike rental shop at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi, open from 09:00 to 21:00 every day. We rent semi-automatic motorbikes, automatic scooters, 50cc motorbikes, electric motorbikes and electric bicycles by the day, week or month. In Vietnamese the shop is known as Cho Thuê Xe Máy Nguyễn Tú, and our Google Maps listing is Thuê xe máy ở Hà Nội - Nguyễn Tú.

This English website is the shop’s guide for travelers, expats and students who want to rent a motorbike in Hanoi. It explains our prices and deposits, helps you choose a bike, and covers the practical side of riding in and around the city.

How renting with us works

  1. Ask first. Call or message us on Zalo at 0942 467 674, or email us, with your dates, the bike you want and how many bikes you need.
  2. Confirm the terms. Before the rental contract is signed, we confirm the deposit, the rental price, the rental period, the condition of the bike and any delivery or collection cost.
  3. Collect and check the bike. Check the brakes, tyres, lights, horn and mirrors, and take photos of the bike before you ride. The step-by-step rental guide covers what to check.
  4. Return the bike. The deposit is refunded under the terms of the rental contract after you return the motorbike and complete the related obligations.

How this site is made

This site is published by the shop, not by an independent review site, so it does not rank or review other rental companies. We follow a few rules so the guides stay useful and accurate:

  • One source for business facts. The address, phone number, opening hours, prices and deposits on every page come from the shop’s own reference data. When a price changes, it changes everywhere at once.
  • No made-up numbers. Where a price is not published, such as weekly or monthly 50cc rental, the page tells you to ask instead of estimating one.
  • Official sources for rules. Guides about licences, helmets and traffic rules link to official sources such as Vietnam’s Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety No. 36/2024/QH15. Rules change, so check the current text before you ride.
  • Clear labels on images. The illustrations on the homepage are AI-generated and captioned as such; they are not photos of our shop or our bikes.
  • Writing tools. Some guides are drafted with the help of AI writing tools. Business facts in them come from the reference data above, and you can report anything that looks wrong.
  • Dates on every guide. Each article shows when it was published and when its text was last updated.

If you find an error, email nguyentuantu8x@gmail.com with the page address and we will check it.

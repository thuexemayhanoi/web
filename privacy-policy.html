Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu (the motorbike rental service behind https://app.rentbikehanoi.com/) handles information when you use this website.

What we collect

This is a static website. We do not run accounts, logins, payment forms or analytics on it. When you contact us by phone, email or the contact details published on the site, we receive only the information you choose to send (for example your name, dates and rental questions).

How we use it

We use information you send us only to answer your enquiry and arrange rentals. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Local storage

The website stores your Light/Dark theme choice in your browser localStorage. It contains no personal data and you can clear it in your browser settings.

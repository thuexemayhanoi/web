Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu (“we”, the motorbike rental shop at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi) receives when you use https://app.rentbikehanoi.com/ or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • This website has no accounts, logins, payment forms, advertising trackers or analytics.
  • We only receive personal information that you choose to send us by phone, Zalo, email or other messaging.
  • We use it to answer you and to arrange and manage your rental. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us.

Using the website

This is a static website. Pages, the price calculator, the article search and the chat assistant all run in your browser. The search loads a list of articles from this website, and the chat assistant answers from a fixed set of replies and an index of this site’s pages; what you type into the search box or the chat assistant is not sent to us or stored by us.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to deliver the site. GitHub’s own privacy statement explains how it handles that data.

Browser storage

The website stores one setting in your browser’s local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. It contains no personal data, is not sent to us, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. The website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

When you contact us

When you call, message us on Zalo, email us or contact us through another channel linked on this site, we receive what you send: for example your name, phone number or email address, the dates you want to rent, the bike you are interested in and your questions. Messaging services such as Zalo, Facebook and email providers process your messages under their own privacy policies.

When you rent a motorbike

If you rent from us, the rental contract records the details needed for that rental, such as your name, contact details, rental dates, the motorbike, the deposit and any identification or security documents agreed with you. We use these details to provide the rental, to return your deposit under the contract and to meet our legal obligations.

How we use information

  • to answer your questions and confirm availability, prices and deposits;
  • to prepare, carry out and close your rental contract, including returning the deposit;
  • to contact you about your rental, for example about the return time;
  • to comply with Vietnamese law and requests from competent authorities.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and rental records only for as long as we need them for your enquiry or rental, to settle any open matter, and for as long as Vietnamese law requires us to keep records.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it, by emailing nguyentuantu8x@gmail.com. We may need to keep some information while a rental contract is open or where the law requires it, and we will tell you if that applies. We handle personal data in line with applicable Vietnamese personal data protection law.

External links

The website links to services such as Google Maps, Zalo, Facebook, Tripadvisor and official government websites. When you open them, their own privacy practices apply. We do not embed maps or social media widgets in our pages.

Children

This website is meant for adults planning a motorbike rental. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way we handle information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email nguyentuantu8x@gmail.com, call 0942 467 674, or see the contact page.

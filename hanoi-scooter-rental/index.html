Riding an automatic scooter in Hanoi

Automatic scooters are the easiest machines to ride in Hanoi traffic. There is no clutch and no gear shifting: you twist the throttle and use the brakes, which keeps your attention on the road instead of the gearbox. That matters in the Old Quarter, where traffic moves in waves and you are braking and rolling forward every few seconds.

A few practical habits make scooter riding in Hanoi much smoother. Wear the helmet that comes with the bike, keep speed modest in dense areas, and expect scooters to filter between lanes at junctions. If you are new to riding in Vietnam, our guide to renting a motorbike in Vietnam covers licenses, road rules and what to check before you set off, and the step-by-step how to rent a motorbike in Hanoi walkthrough covers the rental process itself.

For everyday questions beyond riding — parking, fuel stations and basic upkeep — see the motorbike guides hub. Staying in Hanoi for several weeks? Monthly motorbike rental usually costs less than paying daily rates.

Which automatic scooter is best for your stay?

If the goal is simple city transport at the lower end of the automatic price range, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are the first models to compare. Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade cost more per day and week, so the decision should be based on the bike you prefer and the exact unit available rather than assuming a higher price always means a better fit.

For a short visit, ease of handling and confidence in traffic are usually more important than small differences in specification. For a month-long stay, total rental cost matters more, so compare the published monthly rates in our monthly rental guide before choosing.

Scooter rental for first-time visitors to Hanoi

An automatic scooter removes gear selection from the list of things a rider has to manage, but it does not remove the need for basic riding confidence. Hanoi traffic can be dense, junctions can feel unfamiliar and parking can be tight. If you are new to local traffic, spend the first part of the rental becoming familiar with the brakes, throttle response, mirrors and turning radius before taking on a busy route.

Do not choose a scooter only because it looks easy in a photograph. Sit on the available bike, check that you can place your feet comfortably when stopped and make sure the controls feel natural. A model that suits one rider may feel large or small to another.

What to inspect at pickup

At collection, check front and rear brakes, tyres, lights, horn and mirrors. Look at the bike from each side and take your own photos of its condition. Ask how to open the storage area, where the fuel cap is and how the ignition or lock works on that specific scooter.

These are simple checks, but they prevent confusion later. The How to Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi guide gives a fuller pickup sequence that applies to scooters, semi-automatic bikes, 50cc bikes and electric motorbikes.

Monthly pricing changes the comparison again. For example, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are listed at 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month, while Honda Air Blade is 1,500,000 VND per month and Honda Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND per month.

Using a scooter around central Hanoi

A scooter is convenient for repeated short trips because there is no gear shifting during stop-and-go traffic. The practical questions are where you will park, how comfortable you are at low speed and whether your route takes you through areas that become especially busy at certain times. Leave extra time rather than trying to ride faster through congestion.

If your accommodation is near Hoan Kiem or the Old Quarter, read the Old Quarter guide for local planning. Our shop itself is at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi, so use the location information on the homepage when planning pickup.

When a semi-automatic or 50cc bike may make more sense

Automatic scooters are not the only option. Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are verified at 150,000 VND per day and may suit riders who already like semi-automatic bikes. A 50cc bike is a separate light-city category at 200,000 VND per day. Comparing categories is useful when price, weight or riding style matters more than having an automatic transmission.

The Hanoi Motorbike Rental Guide compares the whole range, and the FAQ answers the most common questions about rates, opening hours, location and availability.

Scooter rental for a week in Hanoi

A week is long enough for the weekly price to matter. Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are 700,000 VND per week, while Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are 1,000,000 VND per week. If you already know you need the bike for most of seven days, compare those weekly figures instead of budgeting from the daily rate.

The weekly rate is still only one part of the decision. Pick the available scooter that feels comfortable and suits your routine. A slightly lower price is not useful if you do not like the bike's size or controls.

Scooter rental for a month

For a month, the price spread becomes more important: Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month, Honda Air Blade is 1,500,000 VND per month, and Honda Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND per month. These are published rates, not estimates.

Long-stay riders should also think about everyday comfort, parking and their normal route. The monthly rental hub brings those planning questions together so the choice is not reduced to one number.

What to ask when a specific scooter matters

If you want a particular model, send the model name and your exact dates when you contact us. That makes the availability question clear. If you are flexible, say that too; it is often easier to compare the scooters that are actually available rather than choosing from a static list first.

Do not treat a model photo or price table as a reservation. The site is designed to provide stable information while stock changes in real time. The FAQ explains this distinction and gives the current contact details.

Scooter or electric motorbike?

The site also lists an electric motorbike, which is priced on request, so ask the shop for a quote for your dates. The price can be compared with Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade, but the practical decision is different because charging and range depend on the actual vehicle and your use.

If you prefer electric, ask about the specific bike before deciding, including its realistic range and how you will charge it.

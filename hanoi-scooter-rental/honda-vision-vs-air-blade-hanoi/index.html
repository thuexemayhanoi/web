Honda Vision vs Air Blade in Hanoi

The Honda Vision and the Honda Air Blade are the two premium automatics in our fleet, and they anchor the top of the scooter lineup: more size, more comfort and more presence than the budget tier. This guide compares them honestly, the verified prices, the real differences, and the situations where each one is the better rental.

Both models have their own price pages, the Honda Vision rental price guide and the Air Blade rental price guide, and every figure here can be re-checked on the rental cost hub.

Here is the complete comparison, using only verified figures. The Honda Vision rents for 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week, and between 1,800,000 and 2,000,000 VND per month. The Honda Air Blade rents for 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week, and 1,500,000 VND per month. The electric motorbike, for completeness, matches the Air Blade tier at 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week and 1,500,000 VND per month.

Read the table once and the shape of the decision appears immediately: the two bikes are identical in price for short rentals and differ only at the month. A day or a week of riding costs exactly the same on either machine, and the premium over the budget automatics, 50,000 VND per day, buys the larger, more comfortable format either way.

That structure is worth pausing on, because it is unusual. Most model comparisons force a trade-off from the first day; this one postpones the financial decision until the rental is long enough for the monthly figures to matter. Short-term riders choose purely on feel; monthly riders choose with arithmetic. The rest of this guide takes both paths in turn.

Where the prices are the same

For day and week rentals, the Vision and Air Blade are financially identical: 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week, on both. Seven daily rentals would cost 1,400,000 VND against the 1,000,000 VND weekly rate on either bike, so the week saves 400,000 VND regardless of which premium automatic you take.

That means the short-rental comparison has no price dimension at all, which simplifies your week considerably. Whatever you choose among the two, you are paying for the premium tier rather than for a brand: the extra 50,000 VND per day over the Honda Click, Yamaha Mio, Wave and Sirius tier is the same on both machines. If you have decided on comfort, the only question left is which flavor of comfort suits you, and that is a question of sitting on bikes, not reading about them.

The practical advice for short rentals, then, is to treat the pair as one option with two available variants. Tell us your dates, ask which of the two is free, and if both are free, come sit on both. The premium tier is the smallest tier in the fleet, so availability, not preference, often makes the decision for you.

Where the prices differ: the month

The month is where the comparison becomes a calculation. The Air Blade's verified monthly rate is 1,500,000 VND, a single confirmed figure. The Vision's verified monthly range is 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND, and the exact figure within that range is confirmed with us for your dates. Depending on where the Vision lands, the monthly difference between the two bikes is somewhere between 300,000 and 500,000 VND.

Thirty stacked daily rentals would cost 6,000,000 VND on either bike, so both monthly rates are a dramatic saving, roughly a quarter of the stacked price. But the saving is larger on the Air Blade: at 1,500,000 VND, the month costs 4,500,000 VND less than stacked days, while the Vision's monthly range saves 4,000,000 to 4,200,000 VND by the same math. The difference is not decisive, but it is real money, and monthly renters notice it.

For a one-month rental, then, the honest structure of the decision is: pay the difference for the Vision if its character genuinely suits you better, or take the Air Blade and keep several hundred thousand dong for everything else a month in Hanoi offers. Both choices are rational; neither is a bargain or a mistake. The monthly motorbike rental hub explains how longer rentals work in practice and what to discuss with us before committing.

Character: two ways to be premium

The Vision is Honda's style-forward automatic, the scooter that balances grace and practicality in equal measure, the one you see carrying everyone from students to executives across the city. Its character is polished and easy: smooth, friendly, and pleasant to look at, with the kind of rounded design that photographs well outside a cafe and disappears gracefully into traffic.

The Air Blade is Honda's sportier, more angular automatic, built with a sharper attitude: more purposeful styling, a firmer feel, and the presence of a machine that takes itself slightly more seriously. Riders who like it tend to like it a lot, and riders who prefer the Vision find the Air Blade more machine than they wanted. Neither impression is right or wrong; they are the two ends of the premium-automatic personality.

In capability terms, both do the same job at the same level: effortless city riding, comfortable carrying, easy parking, and enough presence for the ring roads and longer paved routes out of the city. The choice between them is aesthetic and ergonomic, which is exactly why the sit test decides it faster than any article can. If your rental is short, feel is the whole decision; if your rental is monthly, feel decides it after the arithmetic has been read honestly.

Comfort, size and city riding

Both scooters earn their premium tier the same way: size. Compared with the budget automatics, they offer more substantial seats, roomier positions for taller riders, better wind protection and a generally more settled ride. On Hanoi's longer boulevards and on day trips out of the city, the difference over the 150,000 VND tier is the difference between arriving and arriving fresh.

In the dense Old Quarter core, both bikes are bigger than the ideal tool, and no honest comparison hides that: a light 50cc machine or a small automatic threads narrow lanes with less ceremony. Premium riders compensate with the ordinary habits, modest speed in the lanes, patience at crossings, and parking judgment, and the trade is one most riders happily make: a little more bulk in the lanes for a lot more comfort everywhere else.

For riders carrying a passenger regularly, the premium tier is where two-up riding becomes genuinely relaxed: proper space for two, enough power for the ring roads, and seating that treats the passenger as a person rather than a parcel. Couples and friends touring the city together overwhelmingly choose this tier, and between the Vision and Air Blade they split on style, not on capability.

Longer stays: the arithmetic that decides it

For a month-long stay, the decision framework is worth writing down plainly. Both bikes at 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week and the monthly figures from above. If you ride roughly daily for a month, both monthly rates beat stacked weeks decisively: four weekly rentals would cost 4,000,000 VND on either bike, against 1,500,000 VND for the Air Blade month or the 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND Vision range.

Notice that the monthly-rate question is bigger than the model question. A rider choosing between stacked weeks and a monthly rate saves 2,000,000 VND or more by going monthly, while a rider choosing between the Vision and Air Blade months saves between 300,000 and 500,000 VND by choosing the Air Blade. Structure first, model second, is the rational order for any long stay, and the rental hub covers the structures in detail.

Also worth saying: for some riders the honest answer is the budget tier instead. A month on a Honda Click at the verified 900,000-1,200,000 VND range costs less than either premium month, and for riders doing pure city errands, the smaller bike is not a compromise but a better fit. The premium month buys comfort on top of mobility, and whether that comfort is worth the difference is genuinely personal. Every figure for every tier lives on the price hub, and we will happily talk you through the honest version of this math for your dates.

Frequently asked questions about the Vision and Air Blade

Which is cheaper, the Honda Vision or the Air Blade?

For day and week rentals they cost the same: 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week. For a month the Air Blade is cheaper at 1,500,000 VND, against the Vision's confirmed 1,800,000-2,000,000 VND range.

Which premium automatic is better for two riders?

Both carry a passenger comfortably and have the space and power for relaxed two-up riding around the city. Choose between them on style and availability; there is no capability difference that matters for touring Hanoi as a pair.

Is the premium tier worth it over the budget automatics?

For city errands alone, the 150,000 VND tier is excellent. The premium tier earns its extra 50,000 VND per day in comfort on longer rides, ring-road travel and two-up touring, and the choice is honestly personal.

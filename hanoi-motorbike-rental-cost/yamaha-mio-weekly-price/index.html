Yamaha Mio Weekly Rental Price in Hanoi: Cost Planning Guide

The Yamaha Mio rents at a verified 700,000 VND per week in Hanoi, which averages 100,000 VND per day when the week is used fully. A weekly rental is planned around that number: what the weekly rate buys, how it beats the daily rate, what to check at handover, and when a shorter or longer period is better.

Compare the weekly rate with the 150,000 VND daily rate and the 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND monthly rate, and ask about the deposit, gear and fuel before you budget for a seven-day stay. For other models and prices, see the Hanoi motorbike rental cost guide and the rental FAQ.

The verified weekly rate for the Yamaha Mio in Hanoi is 700,000 VND for a full week on the same scooter. That Mio price is taken directly from the shop's published list, so plan without assuming discounts or packages. The number on the price list is the planning number.

Divided across seven days, the week averages 100,000 VND per day, against 150,000 VND per day at the daily rate. On the Mio, a weekly booking saves 350,000 VND against seven daily payments, making it the standard choice once a stay reaches five days.

The price sits in the entry tier. The Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius and Honda Click share the Mio's prices, 150,000 VND a day and 700,000 VND a week, so you pick a bike, not a price. The step-up tier, Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade and the electric motorbike, rents at 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week.

A weekly rental is also a commitment decision. A week books one scooter for seven days, so be realistic about how many of those days you will actually ride the Mio. A week with four riding days still beats four daily rates, but only barely, and that math belongs before the shop, not after.

What the Mio Buys for a Week

The Yamaha Mio is a compact automatic scooter built around ease. A week of Hanoi riding is exactly its job: twist-and-go simplicity in daily traffic, a light narrow frame that slips into the tight parking rows that pass for parking in Hanoi, and frugality that keeps a week of fuel on the small side of the budget.

The automatic transmission matters most over a week, because a week is long enough for fatigue. Stop-and-go traffic for seven days is easier on a machine with no clutch and no shift timing, and riders new to Vietnam consistently find the Mio the least demanding option in the entry tier.

In short, the Mio is easy transport, not a showpiece. It carries a backpack and modest shopping, takes a passenger for short hops, and will not turn heads at a cafe. For a week of city life the Mio is the right pick, and its weekly rate is the cheapest confirmed way to ride for seven days.

Planning a Weekly Budget

A Mio week has three costs: 700,000 VND fixed rent, a modest amount of fuel, and small extras like parking, a few thousand VND where you pay. The Mio's frugality keeps the fuel line tiny, and nothing else in a city week moves the budget.

Set the week around a realistic rhythm. A typical tourist week is Old Quarter evenings, a Hoan Kiem loop, a Temple of Literature and West Lake day, a market run, and one trip across Long Bien bridge. Each ride is short, and the Mio carries a week of them without complaint. Riders planning bigger loops should confirm the route with the shop first.

Keep a day un-ridden deliberately. Rainy afternoons happen in Hanoi; on the weekly rate a day parked wastes only about 14,000 VND, small enough to ignore. Daily pricing charges you in full for a day off the Mio; the weekly rate simply covers it.

The budgeting mistake is planning seven full riding days. Count on five or six riding days, leave the rest for rain or walking, and the weekly Mio rate still comes out well ahead. A budget with slack survives contact with a real Hanoi week.

The Daily Rate Comparison

The Mio's daily rate is 150,000 VND, so five daily rates total 750,000 VND against the 700,000 VND week. The break-even sits at five days: any stay of five days or longer should be booked weekly, and the shop can convert an ongoing daily rental on request. Ask, because conversion terms belong to the shop.

Below five days the daily rate is the right choice. Two days cost 300,000 VND, three cost 450,000 VND, four cost 600,000 VND, all under the week. The two options suit different plans, so match the option to your calendar.

The comparison mistake is extending daily past the fifth day. Riders do it out of habit, and it quietly costs 50,000 VND per day. Mark the fifth day as decision day and the week takes over at exactly the right moment.

The Monthly Rate Comparison

Expect a Mio month to cost 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND; the precise price depends on the scooter and terms, so check with the shop. Even at the top of the range, a month costs less than two weeks booked weekly, which is a dramatic saving for long stays.

The break-even between weekly and monthly is early: roughly nine to ten days of weekly rentals already approach the monthly figure. At ten days, daily rent would reach 1,500,000 VND, more than any Mio month, so raise the monthly rate with the shop before a second week.

Simple rule for the Mio: under five days pay daily, five to nine days pay weekly, ten or more ask the shop for the monthly price. The Hanoi motorbike rental cost hub puts every model against every period in one place.

Mio Versus Other Weekly Rates

Within the entry tier the weekly price is identical: Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Honda Click and Yamaha Mio all rent at 700,000 VND per week. The Wave and Sirius are semi-automatic underbones with a foot shifter, while the Mio and Click are twist-and-go automatics, so the real choice inside the tier is transmission feel, not money.

The step-up tier costs 300,000 VND more per week. The Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade rent at 1,000,000 VND per week as larger automatics with more storage and stronger delivery, and the electric motorbike rents at the same weekly figure with charging instead of fuel. The premium buys machine, not status.

There is also the 50cc option for riders who want the smallest engine class, at a verified 200,000 VND per day. Weekly prices for a 50cc machine are not published, so ask the shop for a figure for your dates.

The comparison mistake is upgrading without a reason. A city week doesn't need a step-up scooter, and the 300,000 VND you save pays for several very good meals in Hanoi. Upgrade for storage or comfort, not for the brochure in your head.

Deposits, Gear and Extras

The weekly rate is the headline, not the whole transaction. Ask what the deposit is, in what form it is held, and what has to be true at return for it to come back in full. Cash deposits get receipts and card holds get release dates, and both questions take ten seconds each.

Ask what is included with the week: helmets for each rider, a lock, rain ponchos. A week is long enough for Hanoi rain, and included gear beats improvised gear every time. Inclusion is a question, never an assumption, and confirming at handover prevents a mid-week surprise.

Ask early in the Mio week who to call if the scooter has a problem, and what the swap process is if a check fails. A weekly rental is a relationship, and shops that rent by the week expect these questions.

The extras mistake is signing in a hurry. Take two minutes to read the one-page Mio agreement, and check that any spoken promises on deposit, fuel or inclusions appear in writing. Read it in the shade before riding off, and the week starts with nothing unresolved.

When the Week Is the Wrong Rate

Book the Mio by the day for stays of four days or less (under 700,000 VND in total), and from ten days ask for the monthly figure of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND rather than taking a week. For stays of five to nine days, book the week: averaging 100,000 VND a day, it is the lowest confirmed cost of riding a Mio around Hanoi.

It is also the wrong rate when the plan is uncertain. Mention at the shop if your Mio plans might shrink to three days or run to three weeks, so the right rental type is chosen from day one. Uncertainty is a planning input, not an embarrassment.

For a Yamaha Mio week, check availability with Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu and have them compare your dates against the day, week and month prices. Plan the stay realistically and the rate follows: 150,000 VND per day, 700,000 VND per week, or the monthly rate that makes a long stay cheap.

How many days before the Mio weekly rate beats the daily rate?

The step-up tier, Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade and the electric motorbike, rents at 1,000,000 VND per week.

Hanoi Motorbike Rental Cost & Prices

Motorbike rental in Hanoi is inexpensive, and our prices are verified and published. Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click rent for 150,000 VND/day and 700,000 VND/week. Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade and electric motorbikes rent for 200,000 VND/day and 1,000,000 VND/week.

Weekly rates are real weekly rates — not the daily rate multiplied by seven — and monthly rates are ranges that depend on the model and term. This hub explains how rental costs in Hanoi are built up.

Why weekly and monthly rates are not simple multiples

A week of rental is not seven daily rentals — it is a single commitment, and it is priced lower per day. The same logic applies to monthly hire: the monthly figures are ranges because the final rate depends on the model, the term and the arrangement.

Our homepage calculator uses exactly these verified rates, keeps monthly ranges as ranges, and tells you to contact us whenever no verified price exists (for example, weekly 50cc rental).

How to compare Hanoi rental prices

A useful price comparison starts with the same model and the same rental period. A cheap-looking daily price can be misleading if you actually need the bike for a week or a month. Compare the published weekly rate with the daily rate, and compare the monthly rate with the weekly rate rather than assuming one formula fits every booking.

Bike category matters too. Semi-automatic models such as Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are in the lower published price group, while Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade sit in the 200,000 VND/day group. If you want an automatic bike, review the Hanoi scooter rental guide before deciding only on price. If your priority is a light city bike, see the 50cc rental guide.

What to confirm before paying

The price table is real, but a published rate is not the same as a confirmed reservation. Ask whether your preferred bike is available for your exact dates and confirm the final rental period. If a monthly price is shown as a range, the correct quote stays inside that verified range; the website does not invent a single number.

Details that are not part of the verified price table should be confirmed directly. That includes any deposit or document requirement that applies to your situation. The Hanoi motorbike rental FAQ explains what the site can confirm online and what still needs a direct answer from the shop.

Daily prices: useful for short rentals

Daily pricing is the simplest way to compare a short rental. Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are 150,000 VND per day. Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade, the electric motorbike and the 50cc category are 200,000 VND per day. These are published rates, not temporary promotional prices.

If your stay is only a day or two, the daily number may be the most relevant figure. Once the rental becomes longer, switch the comparison to the weekly or monthly column instead of continuing to multiply the daily rate.

Weekly prices: compare the real weekly rate

Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are 700,000 VND per week. Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade and the electric motorbike are 1,000,000 VND per week. The website does not publish a verified weekly rate for the 50cc category, so a longer 50cc quote should be confirmed directly.

A weekly rate is valuable because it makes budgeting clear before pickup. It also prevents a common mistake: assuming that seven daily rentals and one weekly rental are the same product at the same total price.

Monthly prices and published ranges

Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are published at 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month. Honda Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND per month. Honda Air Blade and the electric motorbike are 1,500,000 VND per month.

Where a range is published, the range is intentional. The exact figure should be confirmed for the actual rental rather than selected by the website. For long-term planning and model comparison, continue to the monthly rental hub.

Why the cheapest daily price is not always the cheapest trip

Suppose two bikes have different daily prices but different monthly structures. Choosing only from the daily column can give the wrong answer for a long stay. The useful comparison uses the same rental period and the same type of use. A visitor who wants a simple automatic scooter should compare automatic models with each other before comparing them with a semi-automatic bike they may not want to ride.

That is also why the scooter hub and 50cc hub exist separately from this page. This page answers “what does it cost?” while the category pages answer “which type fits me?”

Availability is not part of the price table

A published price does not mean the model is currently available. Bikes can be rented and returned throughout the day. The site therefore keeps inventory language cautious and asks visitors to contact the shop with their dates and preferred model.

This separation helps prevent a misleading booking experience. The rate can be verified even when stock changes. For current availability, send the rental period and preferred category; for quick policy and pricing questions, see the FAQ.

What the price page does not invent

The table does not add unverified promotions, discounts, deposits, guarantees or special delivery fees. If a number has not been confirmed for publication, it stays off the table. That rule is more useful than filling a page with attractive but unreliable claims.

Before paying, confirm the rental dates, the available bike and any requirement that applies to your situation. The price hub gives you the commercial baseline; direct confirmation completes the rental.

Example comparisons by rental length

For a one-day rental, Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are the lower published group at 150,000 VND, while Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade, the electric motorbike and the 50cc category are 200,000 VND. For one week, the first four models are 700,000 VND and Vision, Air Blade and electric are 1,000,000 VND.

For a month, the comparison changes again. Wave, Sirius, Mio and Click are 900,000–1,200,000 VND; Air Blade and electric are 1,500,000 VND; Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND. A monthly 50cc price is not published because it is not verified.

How this page should be used

Use this page as the site's price source of truth. Category hubs such as Scooter Rental or 50cc Rental can explain which bike type may suit a rider, but the rate itself should match this table. If another page disagrees with the price hub, the inconsistency should be audited rather than silently creating a second price source.

That structure also helps search engines and AI systems understand the site: one page answers the price question, while model and rental-guide pages answer different intents. It reduces cannibalization and makes future content production easier to QA.

When to contact the shop instead of calculating

Contact us when the period is not covered by a verified rate, when a published monthly price is a range and you need the exact figure, or when you need to know whether a model is available. A calculator can compare published numbers, but it cannot confirm stock or a rental condition that is not in the verified data.

For direct answers about what is published and what still needs confirmation, the FAQ is the fastest companion to this price table.

Honda Click Rental Price in Hanoi

The Honda Click is the cheapest automatic scooter in our published rental list. The Click is a compact automatic scooter: no gears, no clutch, no foot lever. You twist the throttle and the bike moves. That simplicity is the main reason renters ask for it, especially riders who have never used a semi-automatic bike and do not want to learn one during a short trip.

The Click is also light and narrow, which helps in the two situations that define riding in Hanoi: dense traffic and tight parking. A narrower scooter fits through gaps and fits into the marked parking strips that line the pavements. For visitors staying in or around the Old Quarter, those two properties matter more than engine specifications.

Finally, there is the price. At 150,000 VND per day the Click matches the cheapest tier in the fleet while being fully automatic, which is a combination many renters assume costs more. If you are comparing categories before comparing models, the Hanoi scooter rental hub lays out the automatic options side by side.

How the periods compare in practice

The weekly rate saves real money. Seven daily rentals of a Click would cost 1,050,000 VND, while the weekly rate is 700,000 VND, a saving of 350,000 VND. Over a month the difference becomes dramatic: thirty daily rentals would cost 4,500,000 VND against a monthly rate of 900,000–1,200,000 VND.

Per day, the weekly rate works out to 100,000 VND, and the monthly rate works out to between 30,000 and 40,000 VND. If your plans include two weeks or more in Hanoi, the period you choose is worth more than the model you choose. Click versus Mio, Vision and Air Blade

Within the automatic range, the Yamaha Mio is the Click's direct rival: it rents for the same 150,000 VND per day, 700,000 VND per week and 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month. Choosing between them usually comes down to availability and which seat feels better, not price.

One step up, the Honda Vision rents for 200,000 VND per day with a monthly range of 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND, and the Honda Air Blade matches the Vision at 200,000 VND per day with a 1,500,000 VND monthly rate. The premium automatics offer larger bodies and different styling, but the Click covers the same core job of city transport at a lower verified price. The 50cc machines and the electric motorbike also sit at the 200,000 VND daily tier; compare them in the 50cc motorbike rental guide if light weight is your priority.

The honest summary: for pure city riding, the Click is the value pick, and the premium automatics are comfort picks. Riders who plan longer trips out of the city sometimes prefer the larger scooters for sustained speeds, and riders on strict budgets are happy with the Click everywhere.

Automatic riding in Hanoi traffic

An automatic scooter removes one layer of work, but Hanoi traffic still demands attention. The flow of traffic is continuous, intersections are negotiated by slow forward motion and eye contact, and vehicles merge constantly. New riders should spend their first rides on quieter streets in Long Bien or the west side before heading into the Old Quarter, where the density peaks.

Three habits carry most of the safety load: ride at the speed of the traffic around you rather than faster, keep your headlights on, and brake early and with both brakes. The Click's brakes are straightforward for a bike this size, and its modest weight makes low-speed control easy. Storage, comfort and daily use

As a compact scooter, the Click offers under-seat storage that fits a helmet and a few small items, which is the pattern for this class. Riders carrying more use a backpack or a top box where fitted. Two-up riding is possible, as it is on most scooters here, though taller riders sometimes prefer the larger seat of the Vision or Air Blade for longer days.

For daily errands, commuting between districts and short evening rides, the Click is completely sufficient. Where it shows its limits is sustained highway riding out of the city, where larger engines are more relaxed at speed. Fitting the Click into a Hanoi itinerary

Think about how a rental bike actually slots into a visit, because the Click's strengths line up with the way most travelers use Hanoi. A typical morning starts with a ride to breakfast, a loop past Hoan Kiem Lake while the streets are calm, and then a run to whatever district holds the day's plan: the Temple of Literature, the museums, the Long Bien bridge area or the West Lake ring. Each of those trips is under half an hour, and each one is exactly the kind of short, low-speed city riding where a compact automatic is the ideal tool.

Afternoons often add errands and market runs, and evenings the question of where to park near dinner. The Click's narrow body earns its keep in all three situations, particularly in the Old Quarter, where the pavement strips fill quickly and a slim bike slots into gaps a wider scooter cannot. Riders staying several days usually settle into a rhythm in which the bike is simply how the city is accessed, and by the second day the rental has paid for itself in time saved versus walking or ride-hailing.

For visitors who want one longer ride, a loop of West Lake is the classic local cruise, and the Click handles it comfortably within the city. Longer routes out of the province are possible on main roads, but if a day trip like Ninh Binh is the plan, discuss it with us so the bike and the period both match the distance.

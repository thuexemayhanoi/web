Automatic Scooter Monthly Rental Price in Hanoi: Cost Planning Guide

Monthly rental is where automatic scooters in Hanoi become genuinely cheap, with verified monthly rates running from 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND for the entry machines up to a 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND band for the Honda Vision. This guide plans a month on an automatic around those published figures: which machines sit in which monthly tier, what a month costs per day, where the monthly rate beats stacking weeks, and what to confirm before a long booking.

We work through the verified monthly rates tier by tier, the per-day arithmetic that makes a month so much cheaper than daily riding, the break-even against weekly bookings, and the questions that protect a long stay from surprises. The Verified Monthly Rates

The monthly price list for automatics has three verified tiers. The entry tier, the Honda Click and Yamaha Mio, sits in a 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND monthly band. The step-up tier holds the Honda Air Blade and the electric motorbike at a flat 1,500,000 VND per month. The premium tier is the Honda Vision, published in a 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND band. All figures come from the shop’s price list, not from this guide’s imagination.

The band structure deserves a word of explanation. Where a monthly figure is published as a range, the exact point inside it depends on terms the shop confirms at booking, so this guide quotes the band and directs the renter to the counter for the final number. Where a figure is flat, like the 1,500,000 VND Air Blade and electric month, it is a single verified rate. Either way, nothing in the budget rests on a guess.

One automatic class member is absent from the monthly list on purpose: the 50cc scooter publishes a verified daily rate of 200,000 VND but no published weekly or monthly figure on the verified price list, so a long 50cc stay is quoted directly by the shop. A renter set on the smallest machine should ask for the current terms rather than assume any monthly figure, and this guide will not invent one.

What a Month on an Automatic Buys

A month is the structure for people who actually live on the bike: expats commuting to work, students riding to classes, remote workers exploring the city between shifts, slow travellers who settle into one district. The monthly rate prices routine, everyday riding at its cheapest, and the automatic class suits exactly that rhythm, because stop-start city traffic is where twist-and-go earns its keep every single day.

The month also removes the rental from the weekly mental ledger. There is no renewal decision every seven days, no counter visit mid-stay, and no drift into paying daily because the plan was never formalized. One booking, one machine, one calendar month, and the rider’s attention goes to the city instead of the tariff.

The honest trade is commitment. A month is not refundable per day, and a quiet fortnight still pays the full rate. That is why the arithmetic below matters: the monthly figures are so far below the daily and weekly structures that the unused days are still the cheapest riding in Hanoi, but only if the stay genuinely reaches the break-even.

The Per-Day Arithmetic of a Month

Divide the verified monthly rates by thirty days and the automatic class becomes startlingly cheap. The entry band of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND works out to roughly 30,000 to 40,000 VND per day, against a 150,000 VND daily rate. The Air Blade and electric month at 1,500,000 VND is about 50,000 VND per day against 200,000 VND. Even the Vision band lands around 60,000 to 67,000 VND per day.

Against the daily rates, a month cuts the effective cost of the machine by two-thirds or more, at every tier. The daily rate buys the freedom to stop, and that freedom is worth something, but it is not worth paying three times the price for a full month. A renter who knows the per-day figures books the month consciously instead of stretching daily rates out of habit.

The comparison also sets expectations for what a month should cost before any counter conversation. A renter holding the per-day figures, roughly 30,000 to 67,000 VND depending on machine, can sanity-check any monthly quote instantly, and the verified bands above are the reference the shop’s own price list provides.

Monthly Against Weekly Stacking

The weekly rates are the natural comparison: 700,000 VND for the entry machines, 1,000,000 VND for the step-up tier. Two entry weeks cost 1,400,000 VND against the 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND monthly band, so by the second week the month is already winning, and by four weeks the stacked total reaches 2,800,000 VND, more than double the band’s upper end. For the entry automatics, the month wins early and overwhelmingly.

The step-up tier crosses even faster. Two Air Blade or electric weeks cost 2,000,000 VND against the flat 1,500,000 VND month, so around the end of the second week the month takes the lead permanently. The Vision crosses around the same point, with two weeks at 2,000,000 VND sitting inside its 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND monthly band, roughly a tie that the month wins decisively from week three onward.

The practical rule is simple: any automatic stay that will clearly pass two weeks should be booked monthly from day one, and a stay that might reach three weeks certainly should. Renting by the week into a month-long stay is the most expensive structure available on the fleet, and it happens purely because nobody did the multiplication in advance.

Choosing the Machine for a Month

The entry automatics, the Click and the Mio, are the cheapest machines of any kind to keep for a month, and for a city month of short hops they are often all a renter needs. Compact, light and simple, they handle the commute, the market run and the evening loop equally well, and the 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND band makes them the default long-stay choice.

The step-up machines earn their 1,500,000 VND flat month through comfort and character. The Air Blade gives the month a bigger, sportier body; the electric gives it silence and charging instead of fuel stops, which suits a fixed routine especially well. At the same flat monthly rate as each other, the pair turns the choice into machine preference, with no price difference to weigh.

The Vision’s 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND band is the premium monthly option, and the renter paying it knows why: the most comfortable automatic on the books, with the storage and refinement for a month of full-time two-wheeled life. The full range of models, with photos, details and every verified rate, is in the Hanoi motorbike rental guide, worth a browse before committing thirty days to any machine.

Planning a Monthly Budget

A monthly budget has the same three layers as any rental, but the layers matter in different proportions. The headline rate is verified and published. The deposit and gear terms matter more over a month than over a day, because a month gives every term time to bite. And the running costs of riding, fuel or charging, tyre wear, the odd wash, scale with use rather than with the rate.

Ask at the counter what the monthly rate includes: helmet terms, the deposit arrangement and its return, and how the machine is serviced or swapped if a problem appears mid-month. Rental policy belongs to the shop, and this guide invents none of it, so the counter conversation is where the month becomes a complete budget rather than a headline figure.

It is also worth aligning the month with the calendar of the actual stay. A month booked for a five-week visit leaves a week to plan, and a month booked for three weeks still beats stacking weeks at the entry tier. The team at Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu can run any set of dates across all the monthly bands in one conversation, which turns the calendar into the structure in a minute.

Questions Before Booking a Month

First, the inclusions: helmet, deposit, and what happens with fuel or charging at handover. A month multiplies small terms, so an inclusion worth 10,000 VND a day is worth 300,000 VND over the stay. Ask, write the answers down, and treat anything unconfirmed as unknown until the counter settles it.

Second, the mid-month situation: what happens if the machine has a problem in week two, whether a swap is part of the deal, and where the machine can be serviced. A month is long enough that something will eventually need attention, and the renter who asked in advance rides through it calmly while the renter who did not discovers policy at the worst possible moment.

Third, the boundary questions: can the month extend, what happens if the stay shortens, and what the return arrangements look like. The rental FAQ collects the questions that come up most often on long bookings, and a renter who has read it arrives at the counter with the full checklist already in hand.

When the Monthly Rate Is the Wrong Choice

The month is the wrong structure for short stays. Below about ten days the weekly and daily rates keep the plan honest, and paying a full month for a week of riding is the most expensive way to rent anything on the fleet. The break-even exists precisely so that the month is booked when it wins, not simply because it is the cheapest headline figure.

The month is also the wrong structure for a plan that is genuinely unstable, a stay that might collapse into a few days or move cities on short notice. The monthly bands price commitment, and a renter who cannot honestly commit should rent by the week and accept the higher per-day cost as the price of freedom. The structure should follow the plan, never the other way around.

Between those boundaries the month is unbeatable: for a stay of roughly two weeks or more on any automatic from the Click to the Vision, the verified monthly rates cut the cost of riding to a third of the daily figures, and the plan picks itself. Frequently asked questions about automatic scooter monthly price in Hanoi

How much does it cost to rent an automatic scooter for a month in Hanoi?

Verified monthly rates run in three tiers: the Honda Click and Yamaha Mio in a 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND band, the Honda Air Blade and electric motorbike at a flat 1,500,000 VND, and the Honda Vision in a 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND band.

Is monthly rental cheaper than paying weekly for an automatic?

Yes, from roughly the second week. Two entry weeks cost 1,400,000 VND against the 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND month, and two step-up weeks cost 2,000,000 VND against the 1,500,000 VND month, so any stay past two weeks should book monthly.

What does a monthly automatic rental cost per day?

Roughly 30,000 to 40,000 VND per day on the entry band, about 50,000 VND per day on the Air Blade and electric flat rate, and around 60,000 to 67,000 VND per day on the Vision band. All are far below the 150,000 to 200,000 VND daily rates.

Can I rent a 50cc scooter monthly?

No monthly figure for the 50cc is published as a verified price, so this guide will not estimate one. Contact the shop directly for current long-term 50cc terms, or compare with the published monthly bands on the Click, Mio, Air Blade, electric and Vision.

Where exactly in the monthly band will I pay?

The entry and Vision monthly rates are published as ranges, and the exact figure depends on terms the shop confirms at booking. Ask the counter for the final number within the band before committing.

What should I confirm before a month-long automatic booking?

The deposit arrangement and its return, helmet and gear terms, the mid-month swap or service policy, and the exact position inside any banded rate. All of those belong to the shop’s rental policy, so settle them at booking rather than assuming.

Honda Vision vs Honda Air Blade: Hanoi Rental Cost Comparison

The Honda Vision and the Honda Air Blade rent at the same verified rates in Hanoi at the short end: 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week each. The difference arrives at the month, where the Vision publishes 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND, confirmed at the counter as a range, and the Air Blade publishes a single 1,500,000 VND. This comparison reads the two step-up ladders side by side honestly, including the quiet surprise that the fleet's performance machine carries the cheaper month.

We compare the daily, weekly and monthly structures, the break-even logic each ladder produces, what each machine's character actually buys, who each one suits, and the deposit, gear and licensing questions that come with the choice. The Vision's ladder is complete and published: 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week and 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND per month, the monthly confirmed at the counter as a range. The Air Blade's ladder is equally complete: 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week and a single published 1,500,000 VND per month, with no monthly range to negotiate.

Nothing in this comparison pads either ladder with invented extras, promotions or bundle discounts, and no missing figure is estimated in place. The two ladders are the entire cost story, and they agree on two of three rungs.

Already the comparison's shape is visible. On the day and the week the machines are indistinguishable in price, so the choice between them is purely a ride question until the stay stretches toward a month. From that point the Air Blade's firm 1,500,000 VND and the Vision's higher range pull the ladders apart, and the calendar becomes part of the decision.

Daily Rate Comparison

On the day rate there is nothing to compare: both machines publish 200,000 VND per day, the step-up tier's shared figure. Four days on either machine cost 800,000 VND, and no amount of arithmetic separates them.

Because the price is identical, the daily decision is a pure ride decision. The Vision is the storage and comfort machine, with a trunk that holds a full-face helmet and a shopping load and a seat that flatters a passenger. The Air Blade is the performance machine, with punch for merging, overtaking and the faster stretches beyond the city core.

The daily mistake is choosing on price where there is no price difference. Riders haggle mentally between two identical daily rates while the real question goes unasked: does this stay carry loads or cover road? The machine that answers that question is the machine to rent, and the daily rate will not help either way.

Weekly and Monthly Comparison

On the week, the two ladders agree again: 1,000,000 VND each, averaging roughly 143,000 VND per day against the 200,000 VND daily rate. Both weeks reward commitment immediately, and both save 400,000 VND over seven paid daily.

On the month, the ladders finally split. The Vision's month runs 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND, confirmed at the counter as a range, while the Air Blade publishes a single 1,500,000 VND. Depending on where the Vision's range lands, the Air Blade month costs 300,000 to 500,000 VND less, and the performance machine turns out to be the step-up tier's monthly bargain.

The structural mistake is assuming the more expensive-feeling machine prices higher everywhere. The Air Blade's bigger engine carries the smaller month, so a long stay that could live on either machine should notice the monthly gap before it notices the engine size. The Break-Even Logic

Both machines share the weekly crossover, because both weekly rates sit at five paid days: five daily rates total exactly 1,000,000 VND, matching the week, and every day after the fifth is money the week keeps.

Monthly crossovers differ, and the difference matters. Two Air Blade weeks cost 2,000,000 VND against the single 1,500,000 VND month, so the Air Blade month wins at ten to eleven days. Two Vision weeks cost 2,000,000 VND against a month starting at 1,800,000 VND, so the Vision's crossover stretches to roughly thirteen days, and a ten to twelve day Vision stay can honestly stay weekly.

The break-even mistake is assuming the cheaper month converts earlier. The Air Blade's 1,500,000 VND pulls its crossover down to ten to eleven days, while the Vision's higher range keeps weekly honest longer, so the machine with the cheaper month is also the machine that wants the monthly conversation sooner. Each ladder keeps its own edges, and the honest planner reads them separately.

Comfort Versus Performance

The Honda Vision is the step-up tier's settled rider: a large under-seat trunk, a roomy seat for a passenger, and a ride that smooths the longer lakeside loops and bridge approaches. It is the machine for stays that carry things and people, and its comfort compounds quietly across a week or a month of city living.

The Honda Air Blade is the step-up tier's urgent rider: a bigger engine that answers the throttle on the ring roads, the bridges and the faster arteries. It earns its character on the days a Hanoi plan leaves the core, and it does the city's stop-start work without complaint the rest of the time.

Within the city both machines do the same work at the pace the streets allow, which is the honest caveat on the performance side. The Air Blade's advantage lives outside the lanes, the Vision's lives inside the loads, and a plan with neither is a plan that belongs on a cheaper tier, not on either of these.

Who Each Machine Suits

The Vision suits the carrier: riders with shopping loads, camera bags or a passenger, and riders whose plan is long lakeside loops and city errands rather than fast road. For that rider, the trunk and the seat decide, and the monthly range is the price of a month of that comfort.

The Air Blade suits the roader: riders with outlying districts, longer commutes or a simple preference for power in reserve. For that rider, the punch decides, and the single 1,500,000 VND month makes a long stay both cheaper and simpler to plan than the Vision's.

The honest boundary between them is one question: what do the days carry? Loads and passengers point to the Vision, distance and speed point to the Air Blade, and a plan that answers neither question should be shopping a tier down, where the daily rate starts at 150,000 VND instead of 200,000 VND.

Deposits, Gear and Licensing

Both machines share the standard counter questions. Ask what the deposit is, in what form it is held, and what has to be true at return for it to come back in full. Cash deposits get receipts and card holds get release dates, and a long-stay hold deserves the answers in writing.

Ask what gear is included: helmets for each rider, a lock, rain ponchos. Hanoi rain arrives fast and a week will likely meet it, so the poncho question stops being theoretical. Confirm the fuel arrangement at handover, full out and full back is the common pattern, and note the Air Blade's bigger engine drinks proportionally more on the fast days.

Licensing is the same question for both, because both are ordinary automatic scooters: riders should check their own licence position against Vietnamese requirements before booking, and the desk can confirm which documents it needs at handover. The rental FAQ collects the common answers on one page.

The paperwork mistake is signing before reading. The one-page agreement at the counter covers deposit, gear, fuel and support for both machines, and two minutes of reading are the cheapest part of either rental.

Choosing Between Them

The honest ladder for choosing is short. Stays of one to four days: pick the machine that matches the days, because the 200,000 VND daily rate is identical either way. Five to nine days: take the 1,000,000 VND week on either machine, again on ride grounds alone. Ten days and beyond: the Air Blade's 1,500,000 VND month makes it the cheaper long stay, and the Vision's 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND month is the price of a month of trunk and seat.

Choose by the real plan, not the hopeful one. A stay that carries loads fits the Vision whatever the length. A stay that covers road fits the Air Blade, and past ten days the calendar joins the recommendation, because the cheaper month belongs to it.

Frequently asked questions about Honda Vision vs Honda Air Blade rental costs in Hanoi

Is the Honda Vision or the Honda Air Blade cheaper to rent in Hanoi?

Identical on the day and week, at 200,000 VND and 1,000,000 VND each. On the month the Air Blade is cheaper: a single 1,500,000 VND against the Vision's 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND range.

When does the monthly rate beat the weekly rate on each machine?

At ten to eleven days on the Air Blade, whose 1,500,000 VND month sits against two weeks at 2,000,000 VND, and around thirteen days on the Vision, whose month starts at 1,800,000 VND, so a short Vision stay can honestly stay weekly.

Which machine should I choose if price is equal?

By the days, not the price. Loads, shopping and passengers suit the Vision's trunk and seat; longer and faster stretches suit the Air Blade's punch. If the plan has neither, a cheaper entry-tier machine answers it better than either step-up.

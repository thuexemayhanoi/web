Honda Click vs Honda Air Blade: Hanoi Rental Cost Comparison

The Honda Click and the Honda Air Blade are two Hondas that live on opposite sides of the fleet's tier line. The Click is the entry-tier automatic, renting at verified rates of 150,000 VND per day, 700,000 VND per week, and 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND per month. The Air Blade is the step-up machine, renting at verified rates of 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week, and a flat 1,500,000 VND per month.

This guide compares those verified figures at every period, prices what the step-up premium buys, and pays special attention to the Air Blade's flat monthly rate, which changes the long-stay arithmetic in a way most comparisons miss. It builds on the guide to Hanoi motorbike rental cost, the main Hanoi motorbike rental resource, and the rental FAQ. Numbers first, tier loyalty second.

Both machines carry the same badge and both are fully automatic, which makes this a comparison the rider can make entirely with numbers. There is no format to learn on either machine, no clutch to coordinate, and no question about which one a beginner can handle. Whichever Honda you take, the riding itself will be identical in kind: twist and go.

What differs is position. The Click anchors the entry tier, the fleet's starter automatics priced for every budget. The Air Blade sits in the step-up tier, the class the shop positions as more premium. The tier line between them is the clearest single decision in the rental list, because everything below the price gap stays constant between the two machines.

That makes the choice honest and comfortable: a renter is deciding how much to spend, not how to ride. The rest of this guide reads the verified rates at each period, explains what the premium is buying, and works through the monthly arithmetic where the Air Blade's flat rate does something its tier-mates do not.

The Verified Rates Side by Side

Daily, the Click rents at a verified 150,000 VND and the Air Blade at a verified 200,000 VND, a standing premium of 50,000 VND per day. On a short visit the difference is small enough to weigh against comfort in one glance, and many renters settle it there and then at the counter.

Weekly, the gap is 300,000 VND: a verified 700,000 VND Click week against a verified 1,000,000 VND Air Blade week. Seven days of the same decision, and by the end of the week the premium is a visible line in any trip budget. Renters planning exactly seven days should treat that as the honest size of the upgrade.

Monthly, the structure changes shape. The Click month is a verified band of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND, while the Air Blade month is a flat, verified 1,500,000 VND with no range and no negotiation. The full pricing structure behind both figures is charted in the guide to Hanoi motorbike rental cost, and the flat rate deserves its own section below.

What the Premium Pays For

The premium buys the step-up tier, which in this fleet means positioning rather than difficulty. The Air Blade is the class the shop places above the starter automatics, the tier renters move to when they want the machine to feel like an upgrade. That is a real product, and the fleet prices it identically at every period.

The premium does not buy capability. The Click already handles Hanoi traffic without ceremony, starts with a twist, and asks nothing of its rider that the Air Blade does not also ask. A renter stepping up is not solving a problem; they are choosing a nicer answer to a question the entry tier has already answered completely.

The honest framing is comfort versus value, with no wrong answers. Renters who ride long days or who arrive with their preferences already formed often find the premium easy to justify. Renters with ordinary city plans usually find the Click's rates easier to enjoy. The verified prices let both camps make the call before the counter, and this guide keeps the framing that neutral on purpose.

The Value Case for the Click

The Click's case is the floor. At 150,000 VND per day it is the cheapest way onto a twist-and-go Honda in this fleet, at 700,000 VND per week it is the cheapest published automatic week, and its monthly band of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND is the value structure every longer rental is measured against.

The Click's case is also completeness. It is the same format as the machine above it, delivered by the same brand, at the starter price. Renters who want simple and cheap are not compromising on anything the Air Blade offers that matters on an ordinary city itinerary; the tier line is about money, not about the ability to reach the next district.

For the typical rental, days or a couple of weeks of riding between the Old Quarter, the lakes, and the errands of a trip, the Click is the correct default. The premium exists to be declined politely by renters whose plans do not need it, and declining it costs nothing but the badge on the step-up tier.

The Step-Up Case for the Air Blade

The Air Blade's case starts with the tier itself. Step-up renters are buying the premium class, and the Air Blade is that class delivered in Honda's sportier flavor. Riders who want their rental to feel like an upgrade, or who simply prefer the machine's character at first sight, are paying for exactly that and nothing less.

The Air Blade's case continues with its published structure. Its rates are fixed at every period, 200,000 VND daily, 1,000,000 VND weekly, and a flat 1,500,000 VND monthly, so the upgrade can be budgeted to the dong before the trip. A renter never has to guess what the tier costs, whatever their calendar looks like.

The Air Blade's case ends with the month. The flat 1,500,000 VND rate is the most accessible long-stay price in the step-up tier, sitting below the other step-up bands in the fleet. For renters set on the premium class who are also staying a while, that flat figure makes this Honda the arithmetic-friendly member of its own tier. The Flat Month Changes the Math

The Click's monthly band runs from 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND, and the Air Blade's month is a flat 1,500,000 VND. The distance between the two is therefore between 300,000 and 600,000 VND, depending entirely on where the Click quote lands inside its band. A renter who asks that single question knows the real size of the upgrade.

Compare that with the wider step-up tier and the Air Blade's position stands out. Other step-up machines in the fleet carry higher monthly figures than the Air Blade's flat 1,500,000 VND, which makes it the entry door to the premium class for long stays. Renters who want the tier but watch the budget should start their monthly comparison exactly here.

The pattern across periods is simple: the premium starts modest at 50,000 VND a day, grows to 300,000 VND a week, and lands at 300,000 to 600,000 VND a month. The longer the stay, the more the flat rate matters, and the more the Click band deserves an exact quote. Matching the Honda to Your Stay

For a short stay, the Click is the default answer. It delivers the full automatic promise at the family's floor price, and the few hundred thousand dong saved over a step-up week covers a good share of a trip's extras. Renters with ordinary plans should start here and only move up if the tier genuinely pulls at them.

For a comfort-driven visit, the Air Blade earns its premium at every published period. Take the verified 200,000 VND daily rate for short stays, or the verified 1,000,000 VND week for a full seven days, and enjoy a tier that was chosen rather than needed. The published structure makes the upgrade impossible to misbudget.

For a long stay, the decision is between the Click's 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND band and the Air Blade's flat 1,500,000 VND month. Ask where the Click quote sits, set the two numbers side by side, and the calendar usually finishes the comparison by itself. Long-stay renters who want the step-up tier will find this Honda the cheapest way into it.

Frequently asked questions about Click vs Air Blade rental costs in Hanoi

Which is cheaper to rent in Hanoi, the Click or the Air Blade?

The Click, at every verified period: 150,000 VND against 200,000 VND per day, 700,000 VND against 1,000,000 VND per week, and a monthly band of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND against the Air Blade's flat 1,500,000 VND. The premium runs from 50,000 VND a day up to 300,000 or 600,000 VND a month, depending on the Click quote.

Are both the Click and the Air Blade automatic?

Yes, both are fully automatic with no gears and no clutch, so riding skill plays no part in this comparison. The decision is purely about tier and price, and either machine is manageable from the first minute for a rider new to Hanoi traffic.

What does the Air Blade's flat monthly rate mean?

The Air Blade month is a single verified figure of 1,500,000 VND, with no band to negotiate inside, unlike the Click's 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND range. It also sits below the other step-up monthly figures in the fleet, which makes it the most accessible long-stay route into the premium tier.

How much more does an Air Blade month cost than a Click month?

Between 300,000 and 600,000 VND. The Click month lands somewhere inside its verified band of 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND, while the Air Blade month is flat at 1,500,000 VND, so the exact gap depends on where the Click quote sits. Ask the shop for both numbers before choosing.

Which Honda should I book for a week in Hanoi?

For value, the Click at a verified 700,000 VND week is the strongest answer. For the step-up tier, the Air Blade at a verified 1,000,000 VND week prices the upgrade at 300,000 VND, which comfort-focused riders on a seven-day visit often find easy to accept.

Does the Click share its price with any other model?

Yes, the Yamaha Mio sits at the Click's exact verified rates of 150,000 VND per day, 700,000 VND per week, and 900,000 to 1,200,000 VND per month, so entry-tier renters can also choose between machines on fit and preference rather than price.

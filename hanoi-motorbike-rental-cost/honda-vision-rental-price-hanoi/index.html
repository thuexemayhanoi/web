Honda Vision Rental Price in Hanoi

The Honda Vision is one of the most requested automatic scooters at our Long Bien shop. It is light, smooth and easy to ride in dense traffic, which makes it a natural choice for visitors who want comfort without a semi-automatic gearbox. This guide covers the verified Honda Vision rental price in Hanoi for every rental period, how the rates compare with the other scooters we rent, and how to book one for your dates.

It is part of our Hanoi motorbike rental cost hub, where every model and period is listed with its verified price.

Why riders choose the Honda Vision

The Vision is an automatic scooter, so there is no gear shifting to think about — twist the throttle and go. That matters in Hanoi, where traffic moves in waves and you stop and restart constantly around the Old Quarter and the bridges over the Red River.

The seat height and overall size suit most riders, including shorter riders who struggle with larger automatics. If you are still weighing automatics against other categories, the Hanoi scooter rental hub compares every automatic model in the fleet, and the motorbike rental in the Hanoi Old Quarter guide covers riding in the busiest part of the city.

Honda Vision rental prices in Hanoi

The verified Honda Vision rental price in Hanoi is 200,000 VND per day, 1,000,000 VND per week, and between 1,800,000 and 2,000,000 VND per month. The monthly rate is a confirmed range; contact us for the exact current monthly price for your dates.

These are the same rates published on our full Hanoi motorbike rental price list, so you can verify any figure before you book.

Daily, weekly and monthly rates compared

Weekly and monthly rates are much cheaper than stacking daily rentals. Seven daily rentals of a Honda Vision would cost 1,400,000 VND, while the weekly rate is 1,000,000 VND — a saving of 400,000 VND in one week.

The gap grows with time. Thirty daily rentals would cost 6,000,000 VND, while a monthly Honda Vision rental costs 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND. If you are staying in Hanoi for a month or longer, start from the monthly motorbike rental in Hanoi hub, which explains how long-term hire works.

Honda Vision vs other rental scooters

The Honda Vision sits in the middle of the automatic range. The Honda Click and Yamaha Mio rent for 150,000 VND per day and are the cheapest automatics, while the Honda Air Blade matches the Vision at 200,000 VND per day.

If budget is the priority, semi-automatic bikes such as the Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius rent for 150,000 VND per day. For the lightest machines, a 50cc motorbike rental in Hanoi rents for 200,000 VND per day. For everyday riding tips and pre-ride checks, browse the motorbike guides hub.

Who the Honda Vision price makes sense for

The Vision is not the cheapest automatic in the published list, so it is worth comparing the rate with how long you plan to rent. At 200,000 VND per day it sits above the Yamaha Mio and Honda Click, which are 150,000 VND per day. Over a week the Vision is 1,000,000 VND while Mio and Click are 700,000 VND. For a month, the Vision range is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND, so a long-stay renter should compare total cost rather than choosing from the daily figure alone.

This does not mean one scooter is objectively better. The useful question is whether the available Vision feels right for your height, confidence and expected use. Ask to see the actual unit available for your dates and confirm availability before building your plans around a particular model.

Pickup checklist for a Honda Vision

When you collect the scooter, check both brakes, tyres, mirrors, lights and horn. Sit on the bike and make sure the available unit feels comfortable at a standstill. Ask how the ignition, storage and fuel access work on that scooter before leaving the shop.

Take a few photos of the bike's condition at pickup. The goal is not to make the process complicated; it is to make the start of the rental clear for both sides. If you have not rented in Hanoi before, the step-by-step rental guide gives a complete collection checklist.

The published weekly price is 1,000,000 VND. That is lower than seven daily rentals at 200,000 VND each. For a month, the verified range of 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND is much lower than paying the daily rate repeatedly. These comparisons are why the correct rental period matters as much as the model itself.

If your stay is long enough to consider monthly rental, compare the Vision against Honda Air Blade, Yamaha Mio, Honda Click and the semi-automatic models in the monthly rental hub. The exact Vision quote within its published range should still be confirmed for your dates.

Honda Vision and central Hanoi use

Many renters choose an automatic scooter because they want simple controls during repeated city trips. Whatever model you choose, plan for low-speed manoeuvring, parking and dense traffic rather than judging the bike only by engine performance.

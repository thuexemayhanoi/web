Electric Motorbike vs 50cc Scooter: Hanoi Rental Cost Comparison

Two machines sit at the easy-entry end of this fleet: the electric motorbike and the 50cc scooter. At Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu the 50cc rents at a published 200,000 VND per day. The electric motorbike has no published price and is quoted on request for any period. Both suit renters who want the simplest possible ride, and the cost comparison starts with one published figure and one quote.

On paper, the 50cc class has a single published daily rate and a published deposit; the electric motorbike’s prices, and any 50cc rental longer than a day, are questions for the shop. Renters who want the simplest possible machine in Hanoi tend to arrive at the same two doors: the electric motorbike and the 50cc scooter. One removes gears and petrol station visits from the riding day, the other keeps things small and familiar. At Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu the 50cc door has a published price of 200,000 VND per day. The electric door opens with a quote, which you request from the shop for your dates and model.

The difference is in the shape of the two rate cards. The 50cc card publishes a daily rate and nothing beyond it, with weekly and monthly prices on request. The electric card publishes no figures at all: day, week and month are all quoted on request, and the deposit is confirmed by model. So a one-day renter compares a published 50cc figure with an electric quote, and a longer-stay renter compares two quotes.

Running costs, charging times, speeds and fuel economy do not appear on a rental price list, so ask the shop about them directly. What the published list does give you is the 50cc day rate and deposit, and the length of your stay decides how far those figures take you.

The published rates side by side

The electric motorbike’s card has no published figures. Daily, weekly and monthly prices are given on request, and the deposit is confirmed by model, so contact the shop with your dates and ask which model you will get. Ask for the quote in writing, with the deposit, the charging arrangement and the condition of the machine noted, so you can budget before leaving home.

The 50cc card reads 200,000 VND per day, and that is the whole of what is published. There is no verified weekly or monthly rate, and multiplying the daily figure does not create one. For a longer 50cc rental, ask the shop for a quote for your dates.

Side by side, the comparison has a simple shape: one published day rate against a quote, and quotes on both sides after that. On the petrol side, that day rate puts the scooter in the 200,000 VND daily tier alongside the Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade, above the 150,000 VND entry tier of the Honda Click, Yamaha Mio, Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius. Those published petrol rates are the fair yardstick for any electric quote, and the full fleet context for those tiers is charted in the guide to Hanoi motorbike rental cost.

What the day comparison decides

For a short stay, the comparison is mostly about the machines. An electric motorbike and a 50cc scooter are different answers to the same question, what is the easiest thing to ride in this city. The 50cc answer costs a published 200,000 VND per day. The electric answer is priced on request, so a renter weighing them for a weekend should get the quote first and then weigh character.

The practical differences are the ones to ask about rather than assume. How each machine fits your routes and plans, what suits your build, and the licence considerations that bring many visitors to these two classes in the first place: whether a given class suits your situation depends on the licence you hold and where it was issued, and that is a fact about you, not about either machine, so confirm it for your own case.

None of those questions has a price on either card. What changes with time is the number of quotes you need: past a single day, both cards hand the question to the shop, so ask for the 50cc and electric prices for your full dates in the same conversation.

Where the 50cc card stops

Reading the 50cc price list correctly means accepting that it is short. A single published figure is not a hidden fee or a trap; it is a card that stops at the day, and the correct response is to ask the shop directly about anything longer. The wrong response is to multiply: 200,000 VND times seven or thirty gives a number that looks like a weekly or monthly rate but is neither, because nobody with the authority to quote it has done so.

This matters in this pairing because the temptation is strong. With no published electric figures to compare against, renters may be tempted to fill both cards with guesses, and empty rows are silent rather than equal. Where no price is published, ask the shop for a quote for your dates.

By the day, the 50cc has a published rate and the electric motorbike does not; for longer rentals, ask the shop what it offers on both. Renters who love the class lose nothing by asking; renters who plan without asking are not planning, they are hoping, and hoping is not a budget.

The electric card starts with a quote

The electric card has no published tiers at all. Day, week and month are each quoted on request, and the deposit is confirmed by model, so the electric cost is known only once the shop has seen your dates. Ask for all three periods in one request, so you can see how the price changes with the length of the stay.

Deposits follow the same pattern. The petrol scooter’s deposit is a published 4,000,000 VND. The electric deposit is confirmed by model, so ask for it alongside the price quote.

For a renter choosing between the two doors, that is the practical difference. The 50cc scooter is a machine you can rent for a day on a number you already have and for longer on numbers you must go and get, while the electric motorbike needs a quote for any period, from an afternoon to a month.

Matching the card to your stay

A few days: the petrol scooter bills a published 200,000 VND per day. Ask the shop for the electric day quote for the same dates, then let riding character, fit and availability settle the rest. Ask to see both machines, confirm what applies to your licence situation, and choose the one that suits the riding you have planned.

A week or two: both cards need a quote. Ask for the 50cc week and the electric week in the same conversation, for the same dates, and compare the two written figures along with the deposits.

A month or longer: the same applies. Neither monthly price is published, so renters set on either machine for a long stay should ask early and in person, so the answer can shape the choice rather than follow it. Wider long-stay planning context sits in the main Hanoi motorbike rental resource.

The conversation at the shop

One conversation with the shop can cover both machines. For the 50cc class, ask what the shop offers beyond the published daily rate for your intended duration, if it offers anything, and at what price, and ask early enough that the answer can inform the choice rather than follow it.

For the electric motorbike, start with the quote: confirm the model and your dates, ask for the daily, weekly or monthly price that fits the stay, and ask for the deposit for that model, the charging arrangement and the condition of the machine. Then ask the same logistics questions that apply to any rental: the documents the shop expects, and what applies to your licence situation. The general answers are collected in the rental FAQ, and your specific case is a five-minute conversation.

The shop is at Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu, 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Long Bien, Hanoi, open daily from 09:00 to 21:00. Ask to see both machines when both are available, put the 50cc long-stay question in words, and ask for the electric quote for the same dates so the two answers can be set side by side. That settles the whole comparison in one visit.

One published rate, one quote

Set plainly, this comparison is one published figure against a quote. The 50cc scooter rents at 200,000 VND per day. The electric motorbike is priced on request for the day, the week and the month. Once the stay grows, both cards hand the question to the shop.

Neither machine wins on paper. The 50cc class offers a known day rate and deposit, and the electric motorbike removes gears and petrol stops at a price the shop quotes for your dates, so the decision comes after the quotes, not before.

In short: by the day, set the published 50cc rate beside an electric quote; by the week or month, ask the shop to quote both. One published figure, a set of quotes, and a choice that your calendar and riding plans decide.

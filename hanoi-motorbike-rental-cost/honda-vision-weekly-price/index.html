Honda Vision Weekly Rental Price in Hanoi: Cost Planning Guide

A Vision week in Hanoi costs 1,000,000 VND, which is about 143,000 VND a day if you ride all seven days. Here we cover the Vision's weekly price in practice: its value, its saving over day rates, pickup checks, and when another rental length suits you better.

Compare the weekly rate with the 200,000 VND daily rate and the 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND monthly range, and ask about the deposit, gear and fuel before you budget for a seven-day stay. For other models and prices, see the Hanoi motorbike rental cost guide and the rental FAQ.

The verified weekly rate for the Honda Vision in Hanoi is 1,000,000 VND for a full week on the same scooter. The figure is the shop's published price, with nothing added: no promotions, bundles or estimates. The number on the price list is the planning number.

Divided across seven days, the week averages roughly 143,000 VND per day, against 200,000 VND per day at the daily rate. Paying daily for a full Vision week would cost 400,000 VND more, which is why anyone staying five days or longer should default to the weekly rate.

The price sits in the step-up tier. The Honda Air Blade also costs 1,000,000 VND a week, so within this tier you choose a bike, not a price; the electric motorbike is priced on request. The entry tier, Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click, rents at 700,000 VND per week.

A weekly rental is also a commitment decision. A week books one scooter for seven days, so be realistic about how many of those days you will actually ride the Vision. Riding just four days of the Vision week? Compare that with four daily rates before you get to the shop.

What the Vision Buys for a Week

The Honda Vision is a proper automatic scooter built around comfort, and a week of Hanoi riding is exactly its job. The under-seat trunk swallows a helmet or a shopping bag, which matters on days that end somewhere other than where they started. The refined twist-and-go transmission asks nothing in stop-and-go traffic, and the roomier seat settles both rider and passenger over the longer loops.

The automatic transmission matters most over a week, because a week is long enough for fatigue. A week of Old Quarter stop-and-go is easier without a clutch or gear changes, and first-time riders in Vietnam often find the Vision the most comfortable scooter to live with.

In short, the Vision is the easy choice: automatic, economical for its class, light enough for city parking, and better finished than the entry tier. For a week of city living in comfort it is the correct answer at the step-up price, and the trunk earns its keep on day one.

Planning a Weekly Budget

Budget a Vision week in three parts: the fixed 1,000,000 VND rent, some fuel, and small costs such as parking lots, a few thousand VND a time. The Vision's frugality keeps the fuel line small, and nothing else in a city week moves the budget.

Set the week around a realistic rhythm. Most visitors fill a Vision week with Old Quarter nights, a lap of Hoan Kiem, a Temple of Literature and West Lake day, a market run with shopping in the trunk, and a scenic crossing of Long Bien bridge. Each ride is short, and the Vision carries a week of them in comfort.

Keep a day un-ridden deliberately. A rainy Hanoi afternoon happens, and on the weekly rate a parked day costs roughly 143,000 VND of unused daily value, real money on the step-up tier. That is the week's hidden benefit: a rest day costs full price on daily rates, but on the week it costs almost nothing extra. Plan five or six real riding days and leave the rest for weather.

The budgeting mistake is planning seven full riding days. A budget with slack survives contact with a real Hanoi week, and a week with slack still beats five daily rates.

The Daily Rate Comparison

At 200,000 VND a day, five Vision days add up to exactly 1,000,000 VND, the same as the week. The week pays off from five days; if you started on daily rates, ask the shop about switching your Vision to a weekly rental. Ask, because conversion terms belong to the shop.

Below five days the daily rate is the right choice. Two days cost 400,000 VND, three cost 600,000 VND, four cost 800,000 VND, all under the week. The two options suit different plans, so match the option to your calendar.

The comparison mistake is extending daily past the fifth day. Riders do it out of habit, and it quietly costs 200,000 VND per day. Mark the fifth day as decision day and the week takes over at exactly the right moment.

The Monthly Rate Comparison

The Vision's monthly rate sits in a verified range of 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND per month, and the exact figure depends on the machine and terms, so confirm it with the shop rather than estimating. Two weeks booked weekly already total 2,000,000 VND, matching the top of the range, so any stay touching two weeks should be a monthly conversation.

The break-even between weekly and monthly is early: roughly thirteen days of weekly rental already approach the monthly figure. A month at the top of the range averages about 67,000 VND per day, and at the bottom about 60,000 VND, both far under the weekly average of roughly 143,000 VND.

The rule is: under five days pay daily, five to about thirteen pay weekly, and from two weeks onward ask the shop for the monthly figure. The Hanoi motorbike rental cost hub puts every model against every period in one place.

Vision Versus Other Weekly Rates

Within the step-up tier the weekly price is identical: Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade both rent at 1,000,000 VND per week; the electric motorbike is priced on request. The Vision is the storage and comfort choice, the Air Blade is the performance automatic with more punch, and the electric swaps fuel stops for charging. Price does not vote inside the tier; the riding week does.

The entry tier costs 300,000 VND less per week. The cheaper bikes, the Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius with foot-shift gears and the Yamaha Mio and Honda Click automatics, all cost 700,000 VND a week. The step-up premium buys trunk space, refinement and comfort, and riders whose week never uses either should save the 300,000 VND.

There is also the 50cc option for riders who want the smallest engine class, at a verified 200,000 VND per day. Weekly prices for a 50cc machine are not published, so ask the shop for a figure for your dates.

The comparison mistake is upgrading without a reason. A week of city lanes does not need under-seat trunk space, and 300,000 VND buys several very good meals in Hanoi. Upgrade for storage or comfort, not for the brochure in your head.

Deposits, Gear and Extras

The weekly rate is the headline, not the whole transaction. Clarify the deposit for the Vision week up front: the amount, the form it takes, and what you must do at return to get it all back. A step-up machine may carry a different deposit conversation than an entry-tier one, so ask rather than assume. Cash deposits get receipts and card holds get release dates, and both questions take ten seconds each.

Ask what is included with the week: helmets for each rider, a lock, rain ponchos. A week is long enough for Hanoi rain, and the Vision's trunk swallows a poncho without ceremony. Inclusion is a question, never an assumption, and confirming at handover prevents a mid-week surprise.

Sort out support on day one of the Vision week: which number to ring for problems and how a replacement would be arranged. A weekly rental is a relationship, and shops that rent by the week expect these questions.

The extras mistake is signing in a hurry. The Vision's weekly agreement is a single page that takes a couple of minutes; make sure what you were told about deposit, fuel and extras is written there. Read it in the shade before riding off, and the week starts with nothing unresolved.

When the Week Is the Wrong Rate

The week is the wrong structure for stays of four days or fewer, where the daily rate totals less than 1,000,000 VND, and for stays touching two weeks, where the monthly range of 1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND should be confirmed instead. Between those boundaries, five days to about thirteen, the week is exactly right, and the roughly 143,000 VND daily average is the cheapest verified way to ride the Vision short of a month.

It is also the wrong rate when the plan is uncertain. If your Vision trip could drop to three days or grow to three weeks, tell the shop at the start so the rental is set up right. Uncertainty is a planning input, not an embarrassment.

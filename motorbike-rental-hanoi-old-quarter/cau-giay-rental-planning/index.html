The riding here runs on the city's newest arterial layer, the streets are wide and legible, and the district connects east to Ba Dinh and the centre, and outward to the western suburbs, with a calm that the older quarters cannot offer. The district's internal texture suits riders from day one. The modern quarter was built for the age of the motorbike and the car alike, and it shows in the geometry.

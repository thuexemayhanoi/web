Hanoi Motorbike Rental Planning

Choose the Right Rental Before You Arrive

Start here to understand the main bike categories, verified prices and pickup location, then follow the specialist guides when you need more detail.

Start with how you will use the bike

A short city stay, a month-long commute and a day trip outside Hanoi are different use cases. For repeated urban riding, an automatic scooter may feel simpler because there is no gear shifting. Riders who already know semi-automatic bikes can compare Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius. A 50cc bike is another light-city option, while the electric motorbike has its own practical considerations. The Hanoi Motorbike Rental Guide compares these categories without forcing every visitor into the same recommendation.

Compare the correct rental period

Daily, weekly and monthly prices should be compared as separate verified rates. Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are 150,000 VND per day and 700,000 VND per week, with monthly rates of 900,000–1,200,000 VND. Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week; the electric motorbike is priced on request. For the complete table, use the Hanoi motorbike rental cost guide.

If you are staying for several weeks, do not assume that paying the daily rate repeatedly is sensible. The monthly motorbike rental guide shows the verified monthly figures by model and explains which details still need direct confirmation.

Automatic scooter or semi-automatic?

Automatic scooters such as Honda Vision, Honda Click, Honda Air Blade and Yamaha Mio are easy to understand because the controls are simple. Semi-automatic bikes such as Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius suit riders who are already comfortable selecting gears. The best choice is the bike you can control confidently at low speed, park easily and use for the route you actually expect to ride.

For automatic models, the Hanoi Scooter Rental hub compares the published prices and model choices. If you specifically want a light 50cc option, use the 50cc Motorbike Rental guide; its published price is 200,000 VND per day, while longer-term 50cc rates must be confirmed directly.

Pickup location and opening hours

Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu is at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi, Vietnam. Opening hours are 09:00–21:00 daily, and the Google Maps location gives directions.

If your accommodation is near Hoan Kiem or the Old Quarter, plan the route to Long Bien before pickup. Our Old Quarter rental guide explains central-Hanoi planning without promising an unverified hotel delivery time or a branch that does not exist.

What this website verifies and what it does not

This site lists verified business information: address, opening hours, contact details and the current price table for the listed models. It also lists the deposit for each model. It does not show live stock, and any special arrangements are confirmed directly with the shop. Availability changes as bikes are rented and returned, so a model page is not the same thing as a confirmed reservation.

Before travelling to the shop, send your dates and preferred category or model. For common questions about availability, documents, location and pricing, the Hanoi Motorbike Rental FAQ gives direct answers and clearly marks which details still need confirmation.

Prepare for pickup

When you collect a bike, check the basics before joining traffic: brakes, tyres, lights, horn and mirrors. Make sure you understand how the available bike starts, locks and opens any storage area. Take a few photos of the bike's condition so the starting point of the rental is clear.

The detailed How to Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi article turns this into a step-by-step process. Practical riding, parking, fuel and routine checks are grouped in the Motorbike Guides hub.

Planning beyond the city

If your rental is mainly for a trip rather than commuting in Hanoi, bike choice should follow the route. Distance, road conditions, weather, luggage and rider experience can all change what feels suitable. The Hanoi Motorbike Trips hub is the place for route-planning content, while current road or weather conditions should always be checked close to departure.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does motorbike rental in Hanoi cost?

Verified rates start at 150,000 VND/day and 700,000 VND/week for the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click. Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are 200,000 VND/day and 1,000,000 VND/week; electric motorbikes and e-bikes are priced on request. Monthly rental is a verified range starting from 900,000 VND depending on the bike.

Can I rent a 50cc motorbike in Hanoi?

Yes. A 50cc scooter or motorbike rents for a verified 200,000 VND/day. We do not have verified weekly or monthly 50cc prices, so contact us for a quote for longer periods.

Where are you located and when are you open?

We are at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Long Bien, Hanoi, Vietnam, open 09:00 – 21:00 every day. The opening status shown here is based on Hanoi time.

How do I confirm a motorbike is available?

Contact us by phone or email to confirm availability for your dates. We never promise specific models in advance without confirmation.

Do you offer monthly motorbike rental in Hanoi?

Yes. Monthly rental is aimed at expats and long-stay visitors, with verified monthly ranges from 900,000 VND to 2,000,000 VND depending on the model. See the monthly rental hub for details.

How much is the deposit, and is a license required?

The deposit is 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND, depending on the motorbike: for example 2,000,000 VND for a Honda Click or Yamaha Mio, 3,000,000 VND for a Honda Air Blade and 4,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND for a Honda Vision. The deposit is refunded under the rental contract after you return the bike. License rules depend on the bike and your documents, so check the current rules before you ride.

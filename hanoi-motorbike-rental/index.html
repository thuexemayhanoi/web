Hanoi Motorbike Rental Guide

Renting a motorbike in Hanoi is one of the most practical ways to explore the city and its surroundings. Our rental shop in Long Bien offers semi-automatic bikes, automatic scooters, 50cc motorbikes and electric motorbikes with daily, weekly and monthly rental options. Prices start at 150,000 VND per day for the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click.

This hub is the starting point of our Hanoi motorbike rental guide library. From here you can reach every topic: rental prices, scooters, 50cc bikes, areas like the Old Quarter, monthly hire and motorbike trips out of the city.

Hanoi is a city built for two wheels. Distances between the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem, Tay Ho, Cau Giay and Long Bien are short, traffic moves at low speeds, and parking a motorbike is far easier than finding space for a car.

For visitors, a rental motorbike means freedom from ride-hailing waits and fixed tour schedules. For expats, it is the everyday commuting default. Both start with the same question: which bike, for how long, at what verified price.

What you can rent

Our lineup covers the common rental categories in Hanoi. Semi-automatic bikes such as the Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are simple, tough and cheap to run. Automatic scooters such as the Honda Vision, Honda Click, Honda Air Blade and Yamaha Mio are the easiest to ride in stop-and-go traffic.

We also rent 50cc motorbikes for riders who want the lightest possible machine, and electric motorbikes for quiet, low-emission city travel. Availability changes — always contact us to confirm the bike you want for your dates.

Choose a bike for your riding style

The best rental motorbike is the one that matches how you actually plan to ride. A Honda Wave or Yamaha Sirius is a straightforward semi-automatic choice for riders who already understand foot-operated gears and want a simple, economical machine. If you prefer twist-and-go riding in stop-and-go city traffic, see our Hanoi scooter rental guide for automatic options such as Honda Vision, Honda Click, Honda Air Blade and Yamaha Mio.

A 50cc motorbike rental in Hanoi is a separate category aimed at light urban use. We also list an electric motorbike option. For every category, availability changes, so the website describes the models and verified prices but does not pretend that a specific unit is always in stock.

Rental periods and verified prices

Rental cost changes sharply with the length of the booking. Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are published at 150,000 VND per day and 700,000 VND per week, with monthly rates of 900,000–1,200,000 VND. Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week; the electric motorbike is priced on request. Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND per month and Air Blade is 1,500,000 VND per month; the electric motorbike is priced on request.

Do not calculate weekly or monthly prices by multiplying the daily rate. The published weekly and monthly figures are their own verified rates. The Hanoi motorbike rental cost guide keeps the complete price table in one place, while the monthly rental guide explains longer-term options.

Pickup in Long Bien and access from central Hanoi

Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu is at 112 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi, with published opening hours of 09:00–21:00 daily. Long Bien sits just east of central Hanoi and is practical for riders heading toward the Old Quarter, the Red River bridges or routes leaving the city to the east and north.

If you are staying near Hoan Kiem or the Old Quarter, review our Motorbike Rental Hanoi Old Quarter guide before setting out. It explains the relationship between the shop location and central Hanoi without claiming an unverified delivery time or guaranteed hotel delivery.

What to confirm before renting

Before you travel to the shop, confirm your dates, preferred bike category and current availability. Requirements such as deposits, identification or licensing can vary by rider and situation, so confirm those details directly.

At pickup, check that the registration card for the bike matches its number plate, and read what the agreement says you owe if the bike is stolen or damaged. If your dates fall around Tet (Lunar New Year), when many Hanoi businesses close for several days, confirm pickup and return days well in advance.

For a practical checklist, read how to rent a motorbike in Hanoi. For short answers about prices, opening hours, location, 50cc rental and availability, use the Hanoi motorbike rental FAQ.

How the main rental categories compare

Our rental information falls into four practical categories: semi-automatic bikes, automatic scooters, 50cc bikes and an electric motorbike option. Semi-automatic Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are priced at 150,000 VND per day and 700,000 VND per week. Automatic Yamaha Mio and Honda Click share those same published daily and weekly rates, while Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are 200,000 VND per day and 1,000,000 VND per week.

A 50cc bike is published at 200,000 VND per day, but no verified weekly or monthly 50cc rate is listed on the site. The electric motorbike has no published rate and is priced on request, so ask the shop for a quote for your dates. Use these figures to compare categories, and ask the shop directly for any rate that has not been published.

Short rental, weekly rental or long stay?

A daily rental keeps a short trip simple. A weekly rental usually makes more sense once you know you need the bike for most of a week, because the verified weekly prices are lower than seven separate daily rentals. A monthly rental changes the economics again: several models have published monthly rates from 900,000 VND.

For longer stays, choose based on both cost and everyday fit. A bike that is comfortable for two days may feel different after several weeks of commuting. Compare the monthly rental hub with the price hub before deciding.

Renting for city use

City use usually means repeated short journeys, slow manoeuvring, parking and dense traffic rather than long uninterrupted riding. That makes confidence at low speed important. An automatic scooter is attractive to many riders because the controls are simple, while a semi-automatic can be a good fit for someone who already uses that style comfortably.

If central Hanoi is your main area, read the Old Quarter rental guide. It covers parking and location planning while keeping the actual pickup location clear: our shop is in Long Bien.

Renting for trips outside Hanoi

A rental used for a longer day trip should be chosen with the route in mind. Distance, luggage, rider experience and current road conditions matter more than choosing the cheapest daily rate. Tell us where you expect to ride so we can discuss the available category rather than assuming every bike fits every trip.

The Hanoi Motorbike Trips hub contains route-planning content. Because road and weather conditions change, use that hub for planning structure and check live conditions separately before departure.

What “verified price” means on this site

A verified price is a rate that has been supplied for publication and is kept consistent across the site. It does not mean the website guarantees a model is available at every moment. Price and stock are separate questions. The same rule applies to monthly ranges: if the published rate is 900,000–1,200,000 VND, the range stays as published rather than being narrowed to one exact number.

This approach also means we do not fill gaps with guesses. If a weekly or monthly rate is not verified, the correct instruction is to contact the shop. That is why the site can remain useful without pretending to be a live booking engine.

Documents, deposits and current legal requirements

Rental requirements can depend on the rider and the situation, so this site does not promise one universal deposit or document rule. Confirm those details directly before pickup. Licensing and road-law questions should be checked against current official Vietnamese information because legal rules can change and may depend on the rider's documents.

The Renting a Motorbike in Vietnam guide starts with a short answer on licences and International Driving Permits and explains how to check the rest against official sources.

Inspect the actual bike, not only the model name

Even when you already know which model you want, inspect the actual unit available. Check brakes, tyres, lights, horn and mirrors. Make sure you know how to start and lock it. Sit on the bike and confirm that you are comfortable balancing it at a stop.

For a fuller handover sequence, use the step-by-step rental guide. The model name helps narrow the choice; the pickup inspection confirms whether the actual rental feels right.

How to use these pages

The homepage gives the short version: bike categories, verified prices, location and hours. The sections below go deeper into choosing a bike, rental periods, pickup in Long Bien and what to confirm before you ride.

If you already know exactly what you need, go straight to the specialist links. If you are still comparing options, start with the category and rental period, then confirm availability. The FAQ is the fastest place for short factual answers.

A simple decision path for first-time renters

First choose the category: automatic scooter, semi-automatic, 50cc or electric. Second choose the rental period: day, week or month. Third compare only the verified price for that category and period. Finally, contact the shop with your dates and preferred model so availability can be confirmed.

This four-step sequence avoids most confusion. It prevents a rider from choosing on price alone, prevents weekly and monthly rates from being miscalculated from the daily price, and makes the availability question specific enough to answer.

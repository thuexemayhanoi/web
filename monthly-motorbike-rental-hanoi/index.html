Monthly Motorbike Rental in Hanoi

Staying in Hanoi for a month or longer? Monthly motorbike rental is the practical choice for expats, students and long-stay visitors. Verified monthly rates start at 900,000 – 1,200,000 VND for the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Yamaha Mio and Honda Click.

Honda Vision monthly rental is a verified 1,800,000 – 2,000,000 VND range, and the Honda Air Blade and electric motorbikes rent for 1,500,000 VND/month. The exact rate within a range depends on the bike and your term — contact us for a firm quote.

Monthly hire means one agreed rate for a month of use instead of paying daily. The bikes are the same models we rent by the day — semi-automatics, scooters, an electric option — just priced for a longer commitment.

Because monthly rates are ranges for several models, the final number depends on the bike you choose and your exact term. We quote before you commit, and we do not publish rates we have not verified.

Monthly rental vs daily rental

The arithmetic is simple: at 150,000 VND/day, daily rental for thirty days would cost far more than the 900,000 – 1,200,000 VND verified monthly range for the same class of bike. That gap is the whole reason monthly hire exists.

Use the homepage calculator to compare any daily, weekly and monthly combination, then contact us to confirm availability and your exact monthly rate.

What to plan for a long rental

For a month-long rental, choose the bike for your everyday routine rather than for one short ride. Think about whether you prefer an automatic scooter or a semi-automatic bike, how far you expect to travel each day and whether you regularly carry a passenger or luggage. The Hanoi motorbike rental guide compares the main categories before you decide.

Before collecting the bike, confirm your exact dates, the model or category you want and what documents or deposit apply to your situation. Those requirements are not guessed on this website. If your stay begins near the Old Quarter, our Old Quarter rental guide helps with location planning; for common questions about availability and rental terms, see the rental FAQ.

Who monthly rental is designed for

Monthly rental is useful when the bike becomes part of everyday life rather than a short sightseeing tool. Expats, students, project workers and long-stay visitors often want predictable transport without buying a motorbike and later having to resell it. A monthly rate puts the main transport cost into one clear period.

That does not mean every long stay needs the same model. Someone commuting across Hanoi may value a different bike from someone making only short neighborhood trips. Start with your routine, then compare the verified monthly prices.

Automatic scooters for a month

Yamaha Mio and Honda Click are published at 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month. Honda Vision is 1,800,000–2,000,000 VND per month, and Honda Air Blade is 1,500,000 VND per month. If you prefer an automatic, the Hanoi Scooter Rental hub explains the model category in more detail.

Do not read a higher monthly figure as a universal quality ranking. The useful choice is the available scooter that fits your comfort, route and budget. Confirm the actual model before making plans around it.

Semi-automatic and electric monthly options

Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are both listed at 900,000–1,200,000 VND per month. They can suit riders who already prefer semi-automatic controls and want the lower published monthly range. The electric motorbike is listed at 1,500,000 VND per month.

For electric use, practical questions such as range and charging depend on the actual vehicle and your routine, so ask about the specific unit available. The website should not guess operational details that have not been verified.

Why there is no published monthly 50cc price

The 50cc category has a verified daily price of 200,000 VND, but this site does not currently publish a verified monthly 50cc rate. Rather than invent a monthly number, the correct instruction is to contact us for a current quote if you specifically want a 50cc bike for a longer stay.

This is part of the site's pricing policy: publish verified figures, preserve ranges when they are ranges and leave unverified gaps unfilled. See the 50cc rental guide for the category itself.

Budgeting a long stay

Transport cost is easier to plan when you separate the rental rate from other personal expenses. Start with the verified monthly rate for the bike, then think about your own fuel or charging use, parking habits and expected travel. Those usage costs depend on how and where you ride and should not be hidden inside a made-up “all-in” figure.

For a pure rate comparison across all periods, use the price hub. It keeps daily, weekly and monthly figures together so you can see when the longer period becomes the sensible comparison.

Pickup and first-day checklist

A monthly rental deserves the same careful pickup as a one-day rental. Check brakes, tyres, lights, horn and mirrors. Make sure the bike feels comfortable at a stop and that you understand the controls, storage and locking method. Take photos of the bike at the beginning of the rental.

The How to Rent a Motorbike in Hanoi guide gives a full step-by-step handover checklist. For a long stay, those few minutes of familiarisation are especially worthwhile.

Extending or changing a monthly rental

Do not assume an extension, model change or other special arrangement is automatic. Availability changes, and any change should be confirmed directly with the shop. The website deliberately avoids promising future stock or a guaranteed replacement bike.

If you already know your stay may change, mention that when you contact us. Clear expectations are more useful than a generic policy that may not fit the actual dates.

Monthly rental versus buying a used motorbike

For long stays, some visitors compare renting with buying. Rental offers a known published cost and avoids the separate task of reselling a bike before departure. Ownership may deserve research for some very long stays, but it brings its own maintenance, paperwork and resale considerations.

Our buying versus renting guide explores that decision without pretending that Motorbike Rental - Nguyen Tu is selling bikes. For common questions about monthly rates and availability, use the FAQ.

Choosing a monthly bike for commuting

For everyday commuting, consistency matters more than novelty. Think about the distance you expect to ride, how often you park in tight spaces, whether you carry a passenger and whether automatic or semi-automatic controls feel more natural. A bike that is easy to manage every morning is usually a better long-term choice than one selected only because it looks more powerful.

If you want automatic controls, compare the scooter hub. If you are comfortable with semi-automatic bikes, Honda Wave and Yamaha Sirius are both in the lower published monthly range.

Changing plans during a long stay

Long stays can change. You may extend, shorten or alter your routine. Do not assume a future model swap or extension is guaranteed; availability can change. Contact us when your dates change so the actual options can be confirmed at that time.

This is another reason the website avoids absolute promises. It publishes stable facts such as verified prices and opening hours, while date-specific arrangements stay direct between the renter and the shop.

Questions to ask before a monthly rental

Before committing, ask which model is available for your start date, what exact monthly rate applies when the published price is a range, and what documents or deposit are required for your situation. If you have a specific daily route or unusual use in mind, mention it before pickup.

For a compact checklist of the most common questions, use the Hanoi Motorbike Rental FAQ. For the full pickup sequence, use the How to Rent guide.
